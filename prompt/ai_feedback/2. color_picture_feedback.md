## Self-check

**How these avoid cliché SaaS gradients / generic heroes**

* The gradients are not random rainbow blobs:

  * They are strictly limited to the **Primordial Soup** and **clean M gradient** with your exact cyan/purple scheme, plus clear compositional rules (giant monogram, left-aligned headlines, white pills). This avoids the “generic teal-to-blue diagonal blob” that every SaaS site uses.

**Potential risk & refinement**

* **Risk:** If the Soup field and large gradient M are used everywhere, the brand could still drift toward “another neon-gradient tech brand” in crowded contexts.
* **Refinement suggestion:** Systematically introduce **more structural cues**—for example, very subtle mono gridlines, tick marks, or bar-notched contours behind the Soup, always in low-contrast lavender—so the visuals quietly reference **data, charts, and music notation**, making Primordial feel more like “creative financial infrastructure” and less like a pure gradient aesthetic.
