## 1) Art direction summary

Primordial’s site should feel like a **music-finance editorial**: a hybrid between an investment memo, a record sleeve, and a data-driven poster. The mood is **calm, confident, and focused**, with controlled bursts of energy coming from the **M monogram** and **Primordial Soup gradients**. The page should feel quieter and more deliberate than a typical startup homepage — more like a thesis you’d want to read than a funnel you’re being pushed through.

**Primary vs secondary color usage**

* **Primary surfaces**

  * Dark surfaces: **Night Purple** (`#2D2246`) and **Soup Shadow** (`#1A132E`) for hero, deep thesis sections, and investor content.
  * Light surfaces: **Paper Lavender** (`#F7F6FF`) for the final “Next Steps” and parts of the footer.
* **Primary accents**

  * **Primordial Purple** (`#7E44F1`) for key headings and the primary button.
  * **Cyan Highlight** (`#6ADDDD`) and the cyan→purple gradient for the **M monogram** and subtle Soup highlights.
* **Secondary accents**

  * **Lavender Mist** (`#BBB0F7`) for borders, grid lines, and muted text.
  * Pink / red-orange are used *only* inside Soup gradients, never as standalone UI colors.

**Typographic hierarchy**

* **Hero headline**
  Founders Grotesk X-Condensed Bold, large, uppercase or small caps, tight leading, in white on dark or deep purple on light.
* **Section titles**
  Founders Grotesk Bold, sentence-case, left-aligned, one line where possible.
* **Subtitles & body**
  Founders Grotesk Regular, medium line-height. Subtitles slightly larger / bolder, body restrained and quiet.
* **Meta & annotations**
  Fira Mono, small, used for eyebrow labels, nav, step numbers, and margin notes.

Compared to generic SaaS, this page should avoid playful icons, 3D blobs, dashboards, and card grids. Instead, it leans on **type, a strict column, and a few strong brand motifs** (giant M, Soup gradients, subtle grids) to create an identity that feels more like a serious, music-native finance brand than a “platform”.

## 2) Section-by-section visual spec

### Global frame

* **Canvas**

  * Content column: `max-width: 720px`, centered, all text left-aligned.
  * Vertical spacing: **very airy** between major sections; each section feels like a chapter.
* **Typography tokens**

  * HeroTitle: `font-display-condensed`, very large, weight Bold.
  * SectionTitle: `font-sans`, weight Bold, size ≈ 28–32px.
  * Subtitle: `font-sans`, Regular/Semibold, size ≈ 18–20px.
  * Body: `font-sans`, Regular, size 16–18px.
  * Meta: `font-mono`, size 12–14px.

**Motif usage**

**Special visual elements**

## 3) Motif rhythm

To keep the page cohesive, the main motifs appear in a **deliberate rhythm**:

* **Gradient M**

  * **Hero:** Giant M Horizon dominating the right side.
  * **Mid-page:** Small M pattern in **For Investors** section, hinting at the M as an atomic unit of the system.
  * **Footer:** Gradient M icon in the footer, possibly plus a faint row of M tiles in the bottom band.

* **Primordial Soup gradients**

  * **Hero:** Soft Soup glow behind the giant M.
  * **Mid-page:** Horizontal Soup ribbon in **For Rights Holders** and faint horizon band in **About**.
  * **Optional:** Very low-opacity background wash in the System section, staying under the grid.

* **Grids / lines**

  * **System section:** Horizontal grid lines across the whole section.
  * **Narrative / About:** Single vertical rule with a `THESIS` label.
  * **Footer:** Optionally, a thin horizontal rule separating the main content from the footer band.

Throughout, all motifs are kept **low-contrast** in text areas: they either sit behind whitespace or are softened until they feel like paper texture. Text always wins.

## 4) Interaction & motion

**Buttons**

* Primary button:

  * Default: background **Primordial Purple**, white text, subtle shadow or 1px outline in a darker purple.
  * Hover: slightly brighter purple or subtle inner glow; no large shadow jump.
  * Active: slight darkening and tiny “press-in” (1–2px translation).
  * Focus: 1–2px focus ring in **Cyan Highlight** at low opacity, outside the border.
* Secondary/ghost:

  * Default: transparent background, text in Primordial Purple or white, 1px border in Lavender Mist.
  * Hover: border color slightly stronger; text maybe shifts to white on dark.

**Links**

* Default: text color in Primordial Purple with a **thin underline**.
* Hover: slightly lighter purple and underline becomes a bit more visible.
* Active: subtle color darkening; no huge transformations.
* Focus: dotted or solid outline surrounding the link or slight background tint.

**PillTag / small interactive labels**

* Default: Paper Lavender bg, Ink Purple text, or dark mode inverse.
* Hover: very subtle background tint change (a touch more purple).
* Focus: 1px outline in Lavender Mist or Cyan Highlight.

**Scroll-related effects**

* **Soft fade-in / slide-up**:

  * Each major section (About, System, Rights, Investors, Next Steps) fades in with a slight upward motion (~12–16px) as it enters the viewport.
  * Animation timing: ~300–400ms, using easing that feels natural, not bouncy.
* **Hero M drift**:

  * Slight parallax-like shift of the giant M:

    * As the user scrolls, the M moves a bit slower than the content (e.g., translateY a small amount).
    * This should be subtle enough to not distract, and implemented in a GPU-performant way (transform only).
* No heavy animations, no scroll-jacking. Everything should be optional sugar on top of a static layout.

## 5) Responsive behavior

**Desktop (≥ 1024px)**

* Content column remains ~720px, centered.
* Hero:

  * M visible on the right.
  * Text column sits left, with plenty of breathing room.
* Margin notes (MetaText) appear in left/right inner margins of the content column.

**Tablet (≈ 768–1024px)**

* Column remains centered but horizontal padding increases to preserve breathing room.
* Hero:

  * M still visible but may be partially cropped; ensure the text area’s dark zone remains solid.
* Margin notes:

  * Might shift to inline labels above the main title when horizontal space is tighter (e.g., `Thesis` line above title instead of side margin).

**Mobile (≤ 767px)**

* Column width constrained to viewport minus 20–24px padding on each side.
* **Stacking / cropping**

  * Hero:

    * The giant M moves **behind** the hero column entirely, treated as a background overlay.
    * Top of the M visible behind the lower part of the hero; the left side remains sufficiently dark for text.
  * Sections:

    * All sections remain **single-column**, with vertical stacking unchanged.
    * Margin notes (e.g. `CREATORS`, `FUNDS & BUYERS`) become small inline eyebrow or captions above the SectionTitle.
* **Typography scale**

  * HeroHeadline shrinks to 1.7–2.0x body size (still in display-condensed), avoiding wrapping too many times.
  * SectionTitles reduce to ~22–24px; body stays ~15–16px.
  * Spacing reduces slightly: section paddings on mobile ~24–32px top/bottom instead of 48–64.
* **Motifs**

  * Soup ribbons and M patterns are simplified:

    * Maintain a hint of gradient or pattern at top or bottom of the section, but avoid busy overlays behind text on small screens.
  * Grid lines in System section may be reduced to just one or two horizontal lines for performance and visual clarity.

## Self-check

**3 ways this spec stays faithful to the Figma design language and avoids generic SaaS aesthetics**

1. **Symbol-first, not product-first:**
   The hero and key sections revolve around the **M monogram** and **Primordial Soup gradients** as core visual motifs — not device frames, dashboards, or icon sets. This mirrors your posters and Figma visuals, not a generic SaaS hero.
2. **Editorial column with margin notes:**
   The single **720px left-aligned column** and use of **Fira Mono margin notes** (`Thesis`, `Creators`, `Funds & Buyers`) give the page a publication/memo vibe instead of a blocky card layout.
3. **Controlled palette & gradients:**
   Gradients are **strictly limited** to the M and Soup fields using the brand cyan/purple scheme; UI elements (buttons, tags, borders) rely on tints and neutrals. That constraint avoids the “rainbow gradient SaaS” look.

**2 weak spots & refinements**

1. **Persona sections (“For Rights Holders” / “For Investors”)**

   * Risk: Two persona sections with bullets and CTA can still feel like a conventional “solutions for X / Y” pattern.
   * Refinement: Introduce one **micro-data annotation** in each (e.g. a tiny sparkline-like line behind a single bullet, or a single small chart axis line) — not a full chart, just a hint of data — so each section feels more like a finance/music note than a marketing panel.
2. **Footer Contact pattern**

   * Risk: A “Contact us + email” footer is structurally familiar.
   * Refinement: Style the steps and footer like a **printed colophon**: give `Session 01: Valuation Workshop` as a small mono caption, align the gradient M like a publisher’s mark, and use slightly more formal copy. That pushes it into “Primordial document” territory rather than a generic B2B sign-off.
