* **How this IA avoids standard “hero + features + testimonials + FAQ + pricing”:**

  * The page reads as a **thesis narrative**—origin → market problem → system → rights holders → investors → “let’s value a catalog”—rather than a big feature grid with icons, testimonials, pricing tables, and FAQ blocks.
  * Both the creator and investor sections are framed as **use cases tied to the valuation rail**, not generic “benefits,” keeping the story anchored in your core mechanism (data + models).

* **Potential risk & refinement:**

  * **Risk:** The “For Rights Holders / For Investors” split is a familiar pattern and could still feel somewhat conventional.
  * **Refinement:** We can give the page a more distinct editorial feel by adding a **single “micro-case vignette”** inside the Primordial System section—e.g., a short, anonymized example of a catalog’s income curve and before/after understanding—written like a two-paragraph story instead of a case-study card. That keeps clarity while making the narrative feel more like a serious memo than a standard marketing layout.
