Brand context:
Primordial is building the financial infrastructure of the creative economy starting with the music royalties market. Our proprietary data pipeline and quantitative model reveal the true value of music,  transforming creative IP from an opaque, intuition driven asset into a transparent, investable class.

Colors:
- Primary purples:
  - Purple 01: #2D2246 (deep night purple, used for dark backgrounds and hero bases)
  - Purple 02: #331862 (slightly lighter deep purple)
  - Purple 03: #7E44F1 (brand accent purple for headlines and primary CTAs)
  - Purple 04: #A080FF
  - Purple 05: #BBB0F7 (light lavender for subtle backgrounds and chips)
- Secondary accents:
  - Black: #000000 (high-contrast text or accents)
  - Cyan: #6ADDDD (bright secondary accent for highlights)
  - Pink: #F869FC (vivid highlight accent)
  - Red-Orange: #E2492F (alert / strong emphasis)
- Each purple has a series of tints (80/60/40/20/10) that can be used for hovers, borders, and soft backgrounds.

Typography:
- Primary typeface: Founders Grotesk (Light / Regular / Medium / Semibold / Bold).
- Headlines: Founders Grotesk X-Condensed in Bold / Semibold with tight leading and large sizes.
- Sub-headings: Founders Grotesk Regular or Semibold.
- Body copy: Founders Grotesk Regular; use Semibold sparingly for emphasis (“less is more”).
- Secondary typeface: Fira Mono for pagination, footers, mini headers, and techy labels.

Visual motifs:
- “Primordial Soup” aurora-like gradients:
  - Deep purple bases with soft, blurred blobs of cyan, pink, and red-orange.
  - No hard edges; everything is smooth and slightly atmospheric.
- White pill UI elements with a colored circular swatch and text label inside.
- Layouts often use:
  - A large left-aligned headline block
  - A big gradient canvas or color field on the right
  - Generous whitespace, minimal decoration beyond gradients and pills.

Overall tone:
- Bold, confident, and editorial.
- Feels like a mix of a music poster and a refined tech brand.
- Not cute, not corporate SaaS; more like a strong visual identity with limited but powerful elements.

Additional brand elements from the logo and key visuals:

- M monogram:
  - The "M" is the core brand symbol. Its shape combines a music note and an upward, chart-like curve.
  - It always uses a clean cyan-to-purple gradient (roughly #6ADDDD → #7E44F1 / #A080FF), with no noise and very soft blending.
  - The M can appear:
    - embedded inside the "Primordial" wordmark as the only gradient letter,
    - as a standalone icon inside a circle,
    - dramatically enlarged as a hero shape that bleeds off the edges of the canvas,
    - or tiled as a low-contrast repeating pattern in the background.

- Logo treatments:
  - Common lockups:
    1) White background, deep purple wordmark, gradient M.
    2) Deep purple background, white wordmark, gradient M.
    3) Pure black background, white wordmark, sometimes with a gradient M.
  - The rest of the letters in "Primordial" are always solid (deep purple or white); only the M gets the gradient.

- Hero composition pattern:
  - Typical layout: huge left-aligned uppercase headline (Founders Grotesk X-Condensed Bold, tight leading), and a large hero visual on the right.
  - The hero visual is either:
    - a giant cropped M shape, or
    - a close-up artist portrait, often with a subtle M pattern in the background.
  - The Primordial logo sits small in a corner as a signature.

- Pattern & background usage:
  - Repeating rows of the M monogram create a subtle background texture.
  - Backgrounds are usually deep purple or black, sometimes with the "Primordial Soup" gradient fields behind the M or photography.

- Photography direction (if used):
  - High-energy, close-up artist portraits with expressive emotion.
  - Subjects are cut out cleanly and placed against deep purple/black + gradient/M-based backgrounds.
  - Large statements sit in clean empty space, never on top of faces.

- Material language:
  - In some applications (e.g. hoodies), the brand pairs deep purple fabric and a large white M with minimal, sleek metallic 3D elements.
  - Overall feeling: bold, music-driven, slightly futuristic, but still minimal and controlled.
